# Hook Patterns, Anti-Clickbait Rules, and CTA Endings

## 12 Hook Patterns

Each entry: **id**, template line, one ELI5 example.
Evidence: hook-window timing is [F, vendor blogs]; the principle "lead with the most interesting thing" is [F, craft consensus].

---

1. **question**
   Template: "Why does [thing everyone knows] actually work that way?"
   Example: "Why can't you just print more money?"

2. **contradiction**
   Template: "[Obvious-seeming fact]. That's the only reason [surprising consequence]."
   Example: "Ice is lighter than water. That's the only reason fish survive winter."

3. **stakes**
   Template: "If [X] vanished, [everyday thing] would [dramatic consequence]. Here's by how much."
   Example: "If bees vanished, your breakfast shrinks. Here's by how much."

4. **mystery**
   Template: "Nobody can explain why [specific verified phenomenon]."
   Example: "Nobody can explain why this spoon-shaped rock keeps rolling uphill."
   *Rule: only use if the mystery is real and paid off in the same video.*

5. **you-were-told-wrong**
   Template: "Your teacher lied about [common school fact]."
   Example: "Your teacher lied about why the sky is blue."
   *Rule: requires a documented source; the correction must land in the same video.*

6. **visual-shock**
   Template: [Extreme or scale-breaking image in frame 1]; narration explains in beat 2.
   Example: First frame shows a car-sized grain of salt; voice says "That is one grain of salt. Here's why."

7. **smallest-to-biggest**
   Template: "This one [tiny thing] holds more [unit] than [vast thing] you can see."
   Example: "This one grain of salt holds more atoms than stars you can see."

8. **challenge**
   Template: "Guess which [A or B]. You're probably wrong."
   Example: "Guess which falls faster: a feather or a hammer. You're probably wrong."

9. **cold-open-result**
   Template: [Show the surprising end state]; voice says "Here's why."
   Example: Show the soufflé collapsing. Voice: "Here's why that happened."

10. **kid-question**
    Template: "A 5-year-old asked me why [thing adults take for granted]."
    Example: "A 5-year-old asked me why the moon follows the car."

11. **number-with-a-face**
    Template: "[Big number] is [tangible physical comparison]."
    Example: "$1 trillion is a stack of bills this tall — [visual shows height vs a skyscraper]."

12. **second-person-scenario**
    Template: "You're [in this everyday situation]. [Surprising problem]. Welcome to [true term]."
    Example: "You're stuck in line at the bank. Everyone panics. Welcome to a bank run."

## Anti-Clickbait Rules

All rules [F, craft consensus; retention penalty for broken promises widely asserted but not rigorously documented]:

- **Pay off the exact promise.** The hook's question or claim must be answered using the same words before the video ends.
- **No promises you can't keep.** If the script can't deliver the answer, rewrite the hook.
- **"You-were-told-wrong" requires a source.** Cite the documented correction. No vague "scientists say."
- **No false urgency.** "You need to know this NOW" — only valid if there's a real time-sensitive reason.
- **Stakes must be real.** Never exaggerate consequences beyond what the evidence supports.
- **Avoid rage-bait setups** (e.g., "this is why you're poor") — off-brand and low-retention after the first watch.

## Endings and CTA Patterns

- **Callback close.** Reuse the hook's exact object or phrase in the last line so the loop closes.
  Example: Hook was "one grain of rice" → final line: "…and that's one grain of rice."

- **Punchline payoff.** End on the satisfying revelation, not on explanation. Silence for 0.3–0.5 s before the final beat adds weight. [F]

- **Seamless loop option.** If the final line flows naturally into the first line, Shorts will loop. [F]

- **One CTA, after the payoff.** Place it after the emotional release, not before.
  Preferred frame: the next open loop — "Next: why prices fall in a recession. Follow to see it."
  Reshare frame: "Send this to someone who thinks [X]." (Reshare is a named Instagram ranking signal.) [E, Instagram]

- **Avoid:** CTAs mid-video, multiple CTAs, "smash that like button," anything that breaks the story before the payoff.
